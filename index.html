From the Heart

A Love Letter

♥

My Miracle,

Before you go any further, slow down. Find somewhere comfortable. Because this is not a message you read once and set aside. This is one you will come back to. Maybe tonight, maybe on a hard day three months from now, maybe the day after an argument when you need to remember what this actually is. Bookmark it in your heart, because you will need it again.

Now. Let me talk to you.

Do you remember July 29th, 2022? I do. I remember it clearly. ODS, a conversation that had no business going the direction it went, and yet somehow, somewhere in the middle of it, I was already talking to you about kids and keyboards and singing together. Not because I had planned to. But because something about you made the future feel like a safe topic. Like it wasn't too much to say. Like you could hold it without flinching.

That was the first miracle. That I could say the things I say those things to you on the first day we met. However, we both disappeared in our own ways and it was very valid.

There was a long stretch after that where the air between us went thin. Weeks turned into months. A conversation here, maybe one there, once every three months if we were lucky. You had told me you were not ready for a relationship, and I heard you. I respected it completely. I did not chase. I did not linger at the door hoping you would change your mind.

Then came the worship session. Small invite wey I invite you o, you crawl back into my life. Scopieeeeee

And somehow the ice of months broke in a single conversation. The words I had spoken to you at ODS, the future we had sketched without permission, started to feel possible again. Like they had been waiting in a holding room all along. But even then, I didn't rush it. Because I knew that the feeling was real but the timing still wasn't ours to force. So I held it again. Carefully. And kept praying.

I knew on the 21st of August 2024, when I cooked for you at my hostel. Peppery stew and rice. Nothing fancy, gisting and doing a couple things and going to ask God about you.

There were months after that where I wrote you mails, numbered, counted-down letters, during a season neither of us fully understood. You might remember some of them. I was praying for me, for you, and for us, with my whole chest, not knowing how it would turn out. And Miracle, you endured. You yielded. You kept your chin up even when the ground felt uncertain. I wrote to you once: "I am not wicked, I dare say I wouldn't be doing this if I don't love you." And I meant it with everything in me, because even then, even before the official beginning, I already knew.

I knew on November 11th, 2024, at Agodi Gardens. Our first date. You refused to eat. I ate both our food. And I was so embarrassingly cheesed up that day that I called Elomiran afterward and told him I didn't know a human being could put this much joy in another person. I wasn't exaggerating. I was genuinely overwhelmed by you.

And I knew on March 11th, 2025, the day I showed up to your parents, took you out, and asked you to be my woman. You said yes. And everything before that day, every prayer, every counted-down mail, every uncertain week, suddenly made perfect sense.

Now let me tell you what this year has been.

It has been the hardest and the most beautiful thing I have ever tried to do. We navigated an LDR, my work schedule that swallowed me whole, your NYSC transition, the distance that sometimes felt like a wall and not just a road between us. There were days I was the angriest of birds. Days I was a swinger of moods. Days I didn't express myself the way you deserved. Days I was running on so little and giving you even less of it, and you stayed. Not out of obligation. But because you chose me too.

I will never forget you writing to me: "We're still here, baby. Still standing, still loving, still fighting for this beautiful thing we're building together." Miracle, that sentence lives in me.

You have superpowers. I said it in December and I will say it again today, louder. I watched you go through things this year that would have broken most people, pressure from work, uncertainty about the future, family, distance, an LDR with a man who wasn't always showing up at full capacity. And you handled every single piece of it with a grace that I genuinely cannot explain. You didn't just survive this year. You built something in yourself while surviving it. Your promotion. Your growth. The woman you are becoming, strong, disciplined, focused, yet so soft and so kind and so present.

I am not shy of you. I am so proud of you it is almost unreasonable.

Here is what I want you to know on this day, one year since the beginning:

I still see the same future I saw when I chose you. It has not dimmed. It has not wavered. Even on the days when things were hard between us, even on the days when I went quiet when I should have spoken, even on the days you wondered if I still felt it, my love for you was not in question. Not once. It was ever-present on silent days. Always beating on cold days. Deeply rooted on stormy days.

I love you for who you are. For your face, yes, I said it, you are recklessly beautiful and it scatters my thinking every single time. For your strength as a first-born daughter who navigates impossible things quietly. For your faith that holds steady even when everything around it shakes. For the way you love God, which is one of the most beautiful things I have ever had the privilege of watching up close. For the way you called a whole village to speak over my life on my birthday, organising it like it was your gift to give, because to you, it was.

I love you for your understanding. The amount of grace you have extended to me to grow as a man is something I do not take lightly. You gave me room to get better. Not everyone does that. You did.

I love you for the way you miss me. For the mails you wrote back, the ones where you said you'd open them slowly because you wanted to savour every word. For "I will marry you tomorrow if I can." For "smiling in finished woman." For choosing me over and over again, even when I made it hard.

This is not just an anniversary message. This is a record. A witness. Proof that on March 11th, 2026, one full year since I asked and you said yes, I am still certain. Still committed. Still choosing you with everything I have and everything I am becoming.

The future I see with you is so clear it almost doesn't feel like imagination. It feels like memory from something that hasn't happened yet. I see us, older, louder, covered in grace, with so much to show for the years we fought through together. I see the home we will build, the prayers, the laughter, the children we already talked about in 2022 like they were already on their way. I see you thriving in ways that will stop people in their tracks. And I see myself right there beside you, being exactly what I promised to be, your number one supporter, your rooter for life, your safe space, your biggest fan.

So, my Nkem. My Achalugo. My Miracle, because that is exactly what you are and what you have been from the very first day.

Happy Anniversary. Happy Birthday. Happy everything that today is.

I love you so sooooo much. And I am just getting started.

Come back and read this again when you need to.

Forever and always,
Your Man. Your Baby. Your Heart. ❤️
